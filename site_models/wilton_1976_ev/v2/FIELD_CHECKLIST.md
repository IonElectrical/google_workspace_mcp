# Wilton #1976 – capture before you leave (10/20/2026)

These items are not in any public dataset: no utility poles, transformers or service data are mapped
for downtown Wilton. Without them, trench lengths and electrical sizing stay "CALCULATED ESTIMATE".
Turn on **location services for the camera / CompanyCam** so every photo carries GPS.

## Every power source (pad-mount, pole, meter, panel)
For each one, take a **close photo of the nameplate or tag**, then a **wide photo from about 20 ft
that also shows a building corner or curb**. The wide shot lets me place it on the LiDAR model to ±1 ft.
- [ ] **Transformer:** kVA, primary/secondary voltage, phase, utility ID number, pad or pole
- [ ] **Pole tags:** every number plate, e.g. "5" and "Wilton Telco 3" at the mill
- [ ] **Meter and main disconnect:** meter number, main breaker amps, voltage/phase on the panel label, spare spaces
- [ ] Ask which electric utility serves each building, and whether a service upgrade has been discussed

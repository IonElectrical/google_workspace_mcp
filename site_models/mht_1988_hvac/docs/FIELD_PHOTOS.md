## What the photos change

- **RTU-5 candidate confirmed as a unit.** IMG_5256/5257 point straight at it (0° off the heading). It sits behind the bridge parapet over the curb canopy.
- **Lightning protection on the bridge roof.** Air terminals stand on the roof edge next to the RTU-5 unit. Ask whether the new units must be bonded to the lightning-protection system (NFPA 780): the RFP text does not mention it.
- **Garage-end bridge unit is a YORK packaged gas/electric RTU** with a unit-mounted disconnect and conduit down the cabinet side (IMG_5255). The bearing points at candidate B2; B1 is also inside the compass error, and the cabinet size reads closer to B1 (13.9 × 11.5 × 6.0 ft) than B2 (9.0 × 7.4 × 3.5 ft). Treat it as B1 or B2.
- No nameplate is legible from the curb. Tags, voltage, MCA/MOCP still need a roof visit (see NEXT_PHOTO_LIST.md).

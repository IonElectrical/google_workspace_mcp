About the data
Measured, estimated, missing
  • measured Ground: USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM from 2019–20 lidar (NAVD88). Roof shapes and heights: 2019 lidar. Building outlines: Overture/OSM, shifted to fit the lidar (0.5 m E, −1.25 m N). Streams: USGS NHD.
  • calc. estimate Stall layouts are proposals. Trench lengths start at a placeholder because no transformer or service is mapped. The dike, bridge corridor and police-exit zone are traced, not surveyed.
  • not loaded FEMA flood zones and BFE, parcel lines, wetlands, orthophoto, utilities. The open sources were blocked from the build machine; load them in Blender with the tools in the .blend.
  • The ground colour is lidar intensity, not a photo. Google 3D Tiles are not in this viewer.
  • Coordinates: NAD83 / UTM 19N (EPSG:26919). Model X = E − 276,000 m, Y = N − 4,747,000 m; heights NAVD88. The north arrow shows grid north, 1.86° off true north here.
  • The browser terrain mesh is 2 m; the measure tool reads ground heights from the full 1 m DEM.
